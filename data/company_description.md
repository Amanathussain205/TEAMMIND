## 2. The product: ShelfSense

ShelfSense is an app for small shops: stock (inventory), billing (point of sale), and a credit book
(khata) for customers who pay later. It has an Android app and a web dashboard.

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| ShelfSense Lite | Free | 1 shop, up to 100 items |
| ShelfSense Standard | PKR 2,500 per shop per month | 1 shop, unlimited items, credit book |
| ShelfSense Pro | PKR 6,000 per shop per month | Several branches, sales reports, staff accounts |

Paying for a full year gives **15% off**. Current version: **v3.2**.

## 3. Company goals (for 2027)

1. Reach **2,000 paying shops by June 30, 2027**.
2. Launch **ShelfSense v4.0 on March 15, 2027**. Its big feature is **Urdu voice search** ("Awaaz").
3. Bring monthly churn (shops that cancel) below **3%**.
